Quick Notes

Income can come from salary, interest, capital gains, dividends, commission, bonuses, gifts, winnings, tips, and rent.

Capital gain = selling price - purchase price. Commission is usually a percentage of a sale. Dividends are paid to stockholders from company earnings. Interest is money a bank gives you for keeping money in an account.
